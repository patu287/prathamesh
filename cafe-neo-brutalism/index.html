01 The idea

Good coffee is a series of small, careful decisions. We make all of them ourselves.

KERN started in a converted print workshop with one grinder and a lot of opinions. Today we roast three times a week, bake every morning and buy from farms we have visited — or will visit.

Every cup follows a simple grid: one origin, one method, one person responsible from bean to pour. Nothing is hidden, nothing is rushed.

    05 How we brew

    1. 01

      Bloom

      Thirty seconds of wet grounds, so the gas can escape and the sweetness can open.

    2. 02

      Pour

      Slow concentric circles at 92 °C. Water temperature checked on every brew.

    3. 03

      Drawdown

      Between two and three minutes. We stop when it tastes right, not when the timer says so.

    “The best cup is the one you finish while it is still warm.” — Mira Keller, head barista
