The story

A little Goa, tucked behind FC Road.

Down GH Patil Path — opposite ICICI Bank, behind Ghole Road — a turquoise door opens into something that does not feel like Pune. Every surface was painted by hand: the gypsy girl with her feather crown and butterfly halo, the teal panels, the frames that climb the wall corner to corner.

We started in 2008 with a small room full of art and a habit of staying up late. Nothing has changed except the queue. Come at 11 AM and it's laptops and cold coffee. Come at 2 AM and you'll struggle to find a chair.

There's a snooker table on the first floor, a shelf of board games and well-thumbed books, and a resident dog or two who may inspect your table. We're self-service — your name gets called when it's ready — and we are open every hour of every day, including tonight.
