Our story

A little Goa in the middle of Shivajinagar.

Tucked down GH Patil Path, just off FC Road, Cafe Flying Gypsy's began as a small room full of art and grew into something closer to a second home. Every wall was painted by hand — the gypsy girl with her feather crown and butterflies, the turquoise panels, the frames that climb corner to corner.

We built the kind of place we wanted at 3 AM: mismatched wrought-iron chairs, a courtyard under fairy lights, a pool table, a shelf of well-thumbed books, and a kitchen that stays on. Come for the coffee, stay for the conversation, or come alone with a book — nobody will rush you.

We're pure vegetarian, pawsitively pet-welcoming, and open 24 hours. Your order is called out by name when it's ready — we're proudly self-service.
